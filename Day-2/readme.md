## 🦠 Malware Types
*   **Virus:** Attaches to files, requires user interaction to open or click.
*   **Worm:** Spreads autonomously across networks (fastest and most dangerous).
*   **Trojan:** Disguises itself as useful software (games, updates).
*   **Ransomware:** Encrypts files and extorts payment (e.g., WannaCry, Colonial Pipeline). Defeated by backups, MFA, and patching.

## 🎣 Phishing & Social Engineering
*   **Phishing:** Fake messages designed to steal credentials or trigger clicks. Includes Spear Phishing (targeted) and Whaling (executives). 
*   **Social Engineering Tactics:** 
    *   **Pretexting:** Inventing a story ("I'm from IT").
    *   **Baiting:** Leaving infected physical media (USB drops).
    *   **Business Email Compromise (BEC):** Hijacking company email threads to alter payment details.

## ⚙️ Technical Attacks & The Attack Chain
*   **Common Vectors:** Password spraying, DDoS (botnets like Mirai), SQL injection, Zero-days, and Supply chain attacks (e.g., SolarWinds).
*   **The Attack Chain:** Recon → Deliver → Exploit → Install → Control → Act. Breaking just one link stops the attack.

## 🛡️ Core Defenses
1. Update systems automatically.
2. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) everywhere.
3. Use a password manager to avoid reuse.
4. Maintain 3-2-1 backups (3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite).
