# Day 1: Why Cyber Security? From Zero to Defender

## 🎯 Overview
Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, data, and people from attacks, damage, and unauthorized access. It spans technology, processes, and human behavior to establish and maintain trust.

## 🔺 The CIA Triad
Every security decision protects at least one core principle:
*   **Confidentiality:** Ensures only authorized individuals can access sensitive data.
*   **Integrity:** Guarantees data remains accurate and unaltered by unauthorized parties.
*   **Availability:** Ensures systems, services, and data are accessible whenever needed.

## 🗝️ Core Security Definitions
*   **Asset:** What you value (e.g., customer databases, credentials, infrastructure).
*   **Threat:** Any entity or event that can cause harm (e.g., hackers, malware, insider threats).
*   **Vulnerability:** A weakness in a system, process, or control (e.g., unpatched software, weak passwords).
*   **Risk:** The probability and potential impact of harm ($Risk = Likelihood \times Impact$).

## 🕵️ Threat Actors & Common Attack Vectors
### Threat Actors
*   **Cyber Criminals:** Financially motivated actors conducting ransomware and fraud operations.
*   **Hacktivists:** Ideologically motivated actors seeking public attention or political impact.
*   **Nation-States:** High-capability state actors focused on cyber espionage and infrastructure sabotage.
*   **Insiders:** Disgruntled, malicious, or careless internal employees.

### Primary Attack Vectors
*   **Phishing:** Social engineering messages engineered to steal credentials or execute payloads.
*   **Malware:** Malicious software including viruses, worms, trojans, spyware, and ransomware.
*   **Password Attacks:** Credential harvesting, brute-forcing, and password spraying.
*   **Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS):** Flooding targeted systems to disrupt service availability.
*   **Web Attacks:** Database and application exploitation (e.g., SQL Injection).

## 🔁 The Security Lifecycle
Security operates as a continuous framework rather than a static goal:  
`Identify` → `Protect` → `Detect` → `Respond` → `Recover` → `Learn`

## 🛡️ High-Impact Defensive Practices
1. Use long, unique passphrases stored in a password manager.
2. Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all systems and primary accounts.
3. Verify sender identities and pause before reacting to urgent requests.

## 📜 Ethics & Legal Compliance
*   Security assessments must be restricted to systems you own or have explicit written permission to audit.
*   Unauthorized system access or probing is illegal under governing frameworks such as the **IT Act 2000**.
*   Hands-on exercises should be conducted exclusively within isolated virtual labs or authorized platforms (e.g., TryHackMe).
